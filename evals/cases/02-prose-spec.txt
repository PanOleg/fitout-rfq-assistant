Tenant fit-out, Level 2, Unit B, Riverside Business Park, Reading

Scope of works (extract from the Employer's Requirements)

The open plan office is to be finished with carpet tiles to the client's selected range,
approximately 520 sqm, laid on the existing raised access floor. The raised access floor
itself is retained and is not part of this package.

Provide 14 no. solid core timber doorsets with vision panels to meeting rooms and offices,
FD30 where shown on the fire strategy drawing, complete with ironmongery set IM-02.

The tea point receives a new run of base and wall units with a quartz worktop, measured
as 1 item, including the sink and tap but excluding the water heater, which is in the
mechanical package.

All new walls are to receive two coats of emulsion on a mist coat: 640 m² in total.
Existing walls are to be made good only.

Lighting: 118 nr LED recessed panels 600x600 with DALI drivers, replacing the existing
fittings on a one-for-one basis.
